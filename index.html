James Clifford

Making things with meaning. I make software, hardware and woodware that tries to leave the world a little better than we found it.

I am drawn to social enterprise and startups where the greatest connections can be made between the work we do and the impact we can have, while working with inspirational people and projects along the way.

I lead engineering at FoodCloud, a social enterprise built on a simple idea: no good food should go to waste when people are hungry. Every meal we redistribute avoids the emissions of growing, transporting and disposing of food that would otherwise be thrown away. Instead we redistribute that good food to local community groups and charities across Ireland, Europe and Africa. We facilitate both environmental and social impact in each transaction on our platform.
